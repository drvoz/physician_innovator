Color · Core

Five colors carry the brand

EquiMED does not have its own palette. It uses the University of Illinois Chicago palette, and it uses it the way the university specifies. Navy Pier Blue is the brand; Fire Engine Red is the accent that makes it UIC. Everything else in this document is structure around those two.

The one rule that decides most layouts

Fire Engine Red never sets body text and never sits on navy. On white it clears 5.42:1, which passes for headlines and standfirsts but violates UIC's own instruction not to set small type in red. On Navy Pier Blue it measures 2.85:1 — below every threshold, for every size.

Red's job is the mark, the rule, the underline, the one number you want counted. When red and navy want to touch, put white between them.

Color · Structure

The navy scale

Tables need banding, charts need a magnitude ramp, and slides need dividers that recede. Rather than admit new hues for that work, EquiMED uses Navy Pier Blue mixed toward white in seven fixed steps. These are structural values, not brand colors — they are never the subject of a layout, and they never replace navy in a headline.

Click any step to copy. Percentages are the proportion of white in the mix. Steps 25 and lighter are fills and rules only — they carry no text.

Color · Supporting

Supporting colors, and the twenty percent ceiling

UIC's secondary and tertiary colors are available to EquiMED for data, diagrams and photographic accents. The university caps tertiary color at twenty percent of a layout, and asks that red and green never sit adjacent without a neutral between them. Both constraints hold here.

Data

Chart palette

Brand colors and chart colors are different jobs. Navy Pier Blue is too dark to sit beside four other series, and Second City Slate is too pale to read as a mark at all. So EquiMED charts use five steps derived from the UIC hues — same families, re-tuned into the lightness and chroma band where categorical color actually works.

The order is fixed. Series one always takes Data Blue, series two always takes Fire Engine Red, and so on down the list. A filter that removes a series never repaints the survivors.

Verified, not eyeballed

This sequence was checked under simulated protanopia and deuteranopia. The closest adjacent pair — Data Green against Data Gold — separates by ΔE 13.0 under protanopia and 20.9 under normal vision, both comfortably above the ΔE 8 target. Data Gold sits at 2.13:1 against white, so any chart using it must carry direct labels or a table view; it is never the only way to identify a series.

Five is the ceiling. A sixth series folds into “Other”, or the chart becomes small multiples. Never generate a sixth hue.

The other three jobs

Sequential — magnitude

One hue, light to dark: Navy 12 → 25 → 40 → 55 → 70 → 85 → 100. Choropleths, heat maps, intensity.

Diverging — polarity

Fire Engine Red arm ← Navy 12 neutral midpoint → Data Teal arm, equal steps each side. Never a hue at the midpoint.

Status — state

Reserved and never reused as a series: #00966C good, #CCB100 watch, #D50032 critical. Always shipped with an icon and a word.

Never

No dual axes. No 3-D. No rainbow ramps. No chart on a navy ground — put it on a white card instead, where this palette was validated.

Identity

Logo and lockup

Both marks are fixed artwork. They are never redrawn, recolored, outlined, reversed to white, set in a single color, stretched, rotated, or given effects. If a background will not accept them, the background changes — not the logo.

Clear space and minimum size

What is and is not permitted

Do

Place the lockup on white, Expo White, or a white containment field over any darker ground.

Do

Scale proportionally from the supplied PNG, and keep the divider rule and both marks together as one object.

Do

Use EquiMED alone inside program materials where UICOMP is already established on the title slide.

Do not

Recolor either mark — including making them all-navy, all-white, or all-black.

Do not

Place either mark directly on navy, on a photograph, or on any fill that reduces contrast against its red and navy.

Do not

Rebuild the lockup by setting the words in a typeface, or alter the spacing between the marks and the rule.

One emphasised mark per slide

Every slide carries exactly one element that is allowed to raise its voice: a figure, a filled row, a highlighted bar, a single word. On white and Expo White grounds that element is Fire Engine Red. On navy grounds — where red is prohibited — it is Chicago Blue, or Amarelo Ouro in the Brazil sub-theme. Two emphasised marks on one slide means neither is emphasised.

This is the motif in place of decoration. No accent stripe down a slide edge, no rule under a heading, no coloured band across a header or footer.

Sub-theme

Brazil

EquiMED's Brazil work — exchanges, Fiocruz collaboration, travel and field decks — gets a sub-theme built on the flag of Brazil. It is a variation on the standard, not a second brand: the layouts, type and logo rules are unchanged, and the ground stays Navy Pier Blue.

Why this works without inventing anything

The blue of the Brazilian flag is Pantone 280 C, #012169. Navy Pier Blue is Pantone 2758 C, #001E62. They measure 1.04:1 against each other — indistinguishable to the eye. The sub-theme therefore keeps UIC navy in the blue role and adds exactly two colors: the green and the yellow.

Rules specific to the Brazil sub-theme

Red retires to the logo

UIC prohibits red and green adjacency without a neutral separator. In Brazil decks, Fire Engine Red appears only inside the UICOMP mark. The emphasised mark that would be red elsewhere becomes Amarelo Ouro on navy, or Verde Profundo on white.

Amarelo Ouro never sets text on white

1.35:1. It is a fill, a rule, a flag bar. On Navy Pier Blue it measures 11.42:1 and becomes the best display color in the whole system — use it there for kickers and KPI figures.

Verde Bandeira is a large-type color

3.83:1 on white clears large text and graphics only. For green body text or captions on white, use Verde Profundo at 5.08:1.

Proportion

Roughly 70% navy and white structure, 25% green and yellow accent, 5% everything else. The deck should read as a UICOMP deck that has travelled, not as a flag.

Brazil chart order

Validated separately — worst adjacent pair ΔE 16.3 under protanopia, 24.5 under normal vision.

Precedence

Where this document and UIC Brand Standards disagree, UIC wins. Where UIC is silent — the chart palette, the navy scale, the Brazil sub-theme — this document governs, and every value in it was derived from a UIC color and measured before it was written down.
