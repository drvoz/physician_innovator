# Physician Innovator Track

Educational infrastructure for the UICOMP Graduate Medical Education Physician Innovator Track — a longitudinal community that develops physicians who can transform healthcare through observation, innovation, leadership, and implementation.

Live at **[drvoz.github.io/physician_innovator](https://drvoz.github.io/physician_innovator/)**

The project is the vehicle. The change in the learner is the most important product.

## The written guides

Two documents govern the track. They are deliberately reciprocal — each contains the same eight commitments, and residents are told to name it when faculty fail one.

- **[Trainee Field Guide](trainee-guide.html)** — what the work is, where it lives, the standing rhythm, the AI covenant, the checkpoint, and what to expect from faculty.
- **[Faculty and SME Guide](faculty-guide.html)** — how to read a resident's repo, how to give feedback without solving the problem, how to run a gate, and where to intervene directly.

Residents start at **[Set up your Notion workspace](notion.html)**.

## Structure of the track

Two years, opening July 2027. A biweekly continuity hour inside existing protected didactic time, alternating between cohort sessions and individual reviews. One two-week focused block per year in the field. Eight progression gates — September, December, March, June — of which December and June carry a written determination to continue.

Between those, the work is asynchronous and written: a weekly field entry in Notion, small frequent commits, and an AI ledger. Observation does not keep business hours; residents working nights and weekends capture then, on a personal device.

## On AI

Residents may use AI and are encouraged to. It occupies one seat and not the other: **partner-organizer, not creative.** Perception, judgment, and decision remain with the learner, and the AI ledger is how ownership is demonstrated rather than policed.

Four times a year the artifacts are closed and the journey is reconstructed out loud. Owned competence gets more vivid under that question. Borrowed competence does not survive it.

## On failure

The only failure is an unlearned experiment. A killed project, an abandoned need, a refused pilot, or a negative result are outcomes this track is designed to produce — never grounds for removal from it.

## Privacy

Nothing identifying a patient, family member, or named colleague belongs in a public repository. Raw field notes stay in Notion; only synthesis reaches a repo. Git history is permanent — deleting a file does not remove it.
